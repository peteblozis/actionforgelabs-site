# Pete Jr. Sales Brief - BuySignal

## Simple Customer Explanation
BuySignal helps people and businesses stop checking prices manually. You tell it what you want, the exact version you need, and the price that makes it worth buying. It tells you when to buy or wait.

## Why It Is Easy To Sell
- People already understand wanting a better price.
- Businesses care about repeated purchasing.
- The first version is simple enough to demonstrate.
- It can start as a setup service before becoming a recurring subscription.

## First Customer Pitch
We built a simple buying-signal tool. Instead of checking prices every day, you enter products you care about and the price that would make you buy. The system tracks the decision logic and is being expanded toward automatic alerts.

## First Revenue Offer
Setup service: $99 to $299 depending on customer complexity.
Recurring monitoring: $19 to $49 per month for small users; higher for business watchlists.
